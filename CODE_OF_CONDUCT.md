# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Omnix a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the project

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment of any kind
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may take corrective action in response to behavior they deem inappropriate. This includes removing, editing, or rejecting comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions.

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces (GitHub, Discord if any, social media when representing the project).

## Reporting

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported via the contact in `SECURITY.md`.
